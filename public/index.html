What does a fractional Forward Deployed Engineer do? +

I work inside your business on one workflow at a time: understanding how the work happens today, then building and running the AI that takes the repetitive handling out of it. Fractional means the time is scoped rather than a full-time hire.

Do we need to replace our current tools? +

No. The work is designed around the inbox, spreadsheet, and CRM your team already uses. Replacing a system of record is a much larger project and is not part of the starting scope.

What happens if the AI gets something wrong? +

Outputs that leave the building pass a human approval gate defined before launch. When the workflow is unsure, it escalates to a person instead of guessing, and every exception is reviewed.

How is pricing decided? +

Prices are agreed on the intro call once the workflow and support level are clear. The labels on the plans page are shown at runtime from the site configuration, so the figures you see are the current confirmed ones.

Who owns the workflow after it ships? +

You do. The workflow, its prompts, and its documentation are handed over, and the Improve level exists for businesses that want the reasoning and tuning handled on an ongoing basis.
